Context-Dependent Reliability of Cell-Type Markers in Single-Cell and Spatial Transcriptomics

MSc Dissertation · Birmingham Tissue Analytics

May–Aug 2026
Research Question

Do cell-type marker genes validated on single-cell RNA-seq remain reliable when transferred to targeted spatial transcriptomics panels, and if not, why?

Methods
  • Curated and statistically validated a 42-marker panel across five immune/endothelial liver lineages using combined log2FC, AUC and visual criteria
  • Independently assessed cross-platform marker transferability to CosMx spatial transcriptomics (1,000- and 6,000-gene panels)
  • Built a full R/Seurat pipeline: QC, clustering, differential expression, cross-platform statistical comparison
Outcome

Identified panel coverage and detection sensitivity, not marker quality, as the primary constraints on cross-platform transfer.

Transcriptomic Analysis of Seasonal Gene Expression in Quercus robur

Group Research Project · University of Birmingham

Oct 2025 – May 2026
Research Question

What is the molecular basis of flowering regulation in pedunculate oak under ambient versus elevated CO₂, using field data from the BIFoR FACE facility?

Methods
  • Preprocessing of raw sequencing data using FastQC, Trimmomatic, HISAT2, Samtools, HTSeq-count
  • Differential expression analysis in R with DESeq2, including seasonal pairwise, CO₂ vs ambient within-month, and juvenile vs adult comparisons
  • Generated volcano plots, upset plots, heatmaps and Venn diagrams; compiled priority gene lists for GO enrichment and BLAST annotation
Outcome

Responsible for most of the bioinformatics pipeline. Work is currently being prepared for publication.
